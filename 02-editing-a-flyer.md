# Chapter 2: Editing a Flyer

## Free Trial vs. Paid Membership

If you have a paid membership to RealtyJuggler, you have access to all 133 flyers. Free trial members can use a sample flyer to see how the flyers work.

> Image: A round yellow starburst badge with the text "Click for FREE TRIAL", a promotional button for the free trial.

## Editing the Text in a Flyer

To edit the text in a flyer, click on the text and start typing. You can copy and paste text from a word processor such as Microsoft Word.

Adobe Reader has a built-in spell checker. Misspelled words have a red squiggly underline. Right-click a misspelled word to see alternative spellings.

To use special characters such as bullets and other unique symbols, paste them into the flyer's text fields.

## Adding Graphics to a Flyer

Click the image you wish to replace. An open file dialog box appears. Find the photo you want to insert on your hard drive and select it. You can insert JPEG, GIF and PNG images into your flyers.

Your images are automatically resized to fit the space available in the flyer template. The agent photo and broker logo are resized proportionally, so there is no distortion. Home photos are resized to fit the space available.

| Image | Aspect Ratio | Scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent Photo | Portrait | Proportionally |
| Broker Logo | Landscape - 2x wider than high | Proportionally |
| Home Photo | Landscape | To Fit |

You do not need to crop or resize your images, because this is done automatically.

## Editing Your Images

You cannot edit your images within Adobe Reader. A number of free image editing programs can crop, rotate, resize and combine your photos. A few are listed at the bottom of the Main:Flyers screen.

## Changing the Realtor and Homes Logos

Two small logos are automatically included in all RealtyJuggler flyers: the Realtor trademark and the Equal Housing Opportunity logo. You can replace either or both with alternatives.

> Image: The Equal Housing Opportunity logo, a black square with a white house outline containing an equals sign, with the words "EQUAL HOUSING OPPORTUNITY" beneath it.

> Image: The REALTOR logo, a black square with a large white capital "R" and the word "REALTOR" beneath it.

If you want to include additional logos (such as CRS), modify your broker logo to include them.

## Creating Your Own QR Code

Several flyers have a space for a QR code. You can change the QR code so that it encodes a telephone number, email address or web page. Searching Google for "QR Code Creator" will show several utilities that can do this. One recommended utility is https://goqr.me/

## Changing the Disclaimer

The bottom of every flyer has a small (6 point type) disclaimer:

"Information contained herein has been obtained from the owner of the property or obtained from other sources that we deem reliable. We have no reason to doubt its accuracy, but we do not guarantee it."

You can edit this disclaimer and change it in any way you wish.

## Clearing Out an Image – Making an Image Blank

Normally you click an image and replace it with the image you want. Occasionally you may want a blank space where the image is. To do this, scroll to the bottom of the list of flyer templates and click the link to the file blank.png. This downloads a completely blank image to your hard drive, which you can use to blank out any image in a template.

> Image: Screenshot of a small "Select Image" dialog with a "File:" text box, a "Browse…" button, and a blue "Clear Image" link underneath.

Newer editions of the flyer include a Clear Image option instead of requiring a blank replacement image.

## Marking Your Flyer with a Just Sold or Price Reduced Sticker / Stamp

Adobe Reader can add a stamp to your flyers. Instructions are at https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/using/adding-stamp-pdf.html

## Different Flyer Designs and Templates

The number of possible flyer designs is endless. If you have a need that the existing flyers do not meet, please let RealtyJuggler know. Part of your RealtyJuggler membership covers upgrades, and the library of flyer templates will keep growing based on customer feedback.
