# Chapter 4: Saving and Printing

## Saving Your Flyer

RealtyJuggler flyers are Adobe Acrobat Reader documents. To save your flyer, choose Save or Save As… from the File menu. If you cannot find this menu item, make sure you are editing the flyer in Adobe Reader. If not, review the Adobe Reader section in Chapter 1.

> Image: Screenshot of the Adobe Reader File menu. The menu bar shows File, Edit, View, Window, Help. The open menu lists Open… (Ctrl+O), Create PDF Online…, Save (Ctrl+S), Save As… (Shift+Ctrl+S, highlighted), Save as Other, Send File, Revert and Close (Ctrl+W).

It is important that you open the flyer using Adobe Reader.

## Printing a Flyer

When you print your flyer, select Page Scaling: Shrink to Printable Area. This optimizes the flyer for your printer, since each printer has a slightly different printable area. If there is too much white space around the edges of your flyer, try Page Scaling: Fit to Printable Area.

### Adjusting White Space around the Flyer Border – Macintosh Users

To fine-tune the white space around your flyers, adjust page scaling or page size.

1. For page scaling, select Page Setup… from the File menu and change the Scale: 100% value.
2. To adjust page size, select Page Setup… from the File menu, then choose "Manage Custom Sizes" under Paper Size. You can then set the non-printable area for your paper.

### Adjusting White Space around the Flyer Border – Windows Users

Printer settings on Microsoft Windows depend on your specific printer and the software that came with it. Most printers allow adjustments to page scaling and/or paper size. These are the settings to adjust to fine-tune the white space for your flyer.

### Taking Your Flyer to a Print Shop

RealtyJuggler flyers can be printed on any printer. You can also save your flyer design and take it to a print shop for professional printing. Printing yourself is faster and economical in low quantities. A print shop produces higher quality results and is economical in quantities of 50 or greater. You can use any print shop; provide them with the saved .PDF file. Have them adjust the margins as described in the Macintosh and Windows white space sections above.
