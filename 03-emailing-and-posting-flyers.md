# Chapter 3: Emailing and Posting Flyers

## Converting a .PDF to an Embedded Image for Email

To send your flyer in an email, upload the file into RealtyJuggler and convert it into an image so it can be embedded in your email:

1. Go to the Main:All Files screen in RealtyJuggler and click the "New Attachment" button.
2. Select your .PDF file from your computer by clicking the "Choose File" button.
3. If you selected a .PDF file, a "Convert to Image" checkbox appears. Select it.
4. Select which page of your .PDF file you want to convert. If your .PDF has multiple pages, repeat this process for each page.
5. Once your images are uploaded, you can embed them into a Mail Form. Video Tutorial 43, on the Main:Tutorials page, shows how.

The same steps work for Adobe Acrobat documents and Microsoft Word documents (DOC, DOCX).

Most email programs do not display images in emails from strangers, meaning senders whose email address is not in the recipient's address book. Your email MUST make sense with images off. Include enough text outside of the flyer to explain what your email is about, assuming the image is not immediately displayed. Encourage everyone you know to add you to their address book, which is the single most effective way to keep your email out of the Junk Mail folder. Spam filters cannot identify the contents of an embedded image, so they flag emails with small amounts of text and large images as an attempt to circumvent the filter. Including sufficient text in all your emails helps the spam filter understand your intentions.

## Adding Canva Designs to a RealtyJuggler Email

If you have a Canva flyer you want to use:

1. Open your flyer in Canva and click the "Share" button in the top-right.
2. Select "Download" and choose a file type, such as a .PNG image.
3. Select the pages you want to download if there is more than one, then click the purple "Download" button.
4. Sign into RealtyJuggler, go to Main:All Files, and upload the file you just saved.
5. Once your images are uploaded, embed them into a Mail Form. Video Tutorial 43, located at Main:YouTube Videos, shows how.

## Converting a Microsoft Publisher Document for Email

If you have a Microsoft Publisher flyer you want to use:

1. Open your flyer using Microsoft Publisher.
2. Go to File > Save As, name the file, and under the file name choose JPEG (or PNG) as the file type. Save the file to your desktop.
3. Sign into RealtyJuggler, go to Main:All Files, and upload the file you just saved.
4. Once your images are uploaded, embed them into a Mail Form. Video Tutorial 43, located at Main:YouTube Videos, shows how.

## Converting a Flyer to an Image Using Zamzar

The free website Zamzar.com can convert almost any file format into an image, including PDF, Microsoft Word and Publisher. Go to the Zamzar website and upload your file. Select .PNG or .JPG as the output format. Follow the Zamzar instructions to download your converted images. Then sign into RealtyJuggler, go to Main:All Files, and upload the image file.

## Converting a Flyer to an Image Using SmallPDF

The free website SmallPDF.com can convert a PDF into a JPEG image. Go to the website and upload your file. Select .JPG as the output format. Follow the instructions to download your converted images. Then sign into RealtyJuggler, go to Main:All Files, and upload the image file.

## Posting Your Flyer to Facebook

1. Go to Main:All Files and click "New Attachment".
2. Pick your .PDF file from your computer by clicking the "Choose File" button.
3. If you selected a .PDF file, a "Convert to Image" checkbox appears. Check it.
4. Select which page of your .PDF file you want to convert. If your .PDF has multiple pages, repeat this process for each page.
5. Check the "Enable Web Link" checkbox and click the link that appears. This downloads your flyer as a PNG image.
6. Sign into Facebook and upload the image you just downloaded. The advantage of using an image is that it does not require Adobe Reader to view.

## Posting Your Flyer to Craigslist or Any Website

1. Sign into RealtyJuggler, go to Main:All Files, and click the "New Attachment" button at the top of the screen.
2. Pick your .PDF file from your computer by clicking the "Choose File" button.
3. If you selected a .PDF file, a "Convert to Image" checkbox appears. Check it.
4. Select which page of your .PDF file you want to convert. If your .PDF has multiple pages, repeat this process for each page.
5. Click the "Download Image" button below the image you uploaded, and save the downloaded image somewhere easy to find on your computer.
6. In your Craigslist ad or other website, select the "Add Images" button and choose the flyer image you downloaded. The file uploads, and your posting now contains a JPEG flyer image.
